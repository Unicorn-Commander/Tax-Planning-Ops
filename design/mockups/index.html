Daylight & Paper

The strategist works the file; you sign the memo. The whole product is one material — paper. You work on a bright daylight bench where every figure sits against its brackets and cliffs; the deliverable is a warm, typeset sheet that floats on it, footnoted and signed. Three signatures: the Strata (the marginal-rate spectrum is the brand), gold = signed (nothing unsigned wears it), and no naked numbers (every dollar opens its engine provenance).
